Quick summary

After completing French classes préparatoires (MPSI/MP), I joined EDHEC in 2024, while simultaneously completing a BSc in Mathematics (L3) at the University of Lille and sitting the entrance examinations for the French actuarial schools, gaining admission to both Dauphine and Jussieu. At the end of that year, I joined EuroCTP as a Quantitative Analyst Intern.

I ultimately chose Dauphine, which I pursued alongside EDHEC's M1 (Lille) and an M1 in Applied Mathematics for Finance at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne — three institutions across two cities, and a year that left little room to breathe.

Two internships followed: a research internship in Machine Learning applied to Astrophysics, and a return to EuroCTP, this time as a Quantitative Researcher Intern.

This year, during my gap year from EDHEC, I will be following the M2 in Stochastic Modelling (Modélisation Aléatoire, DEA Laure Elie), jointly run by Université Paris Cité and ENSAE.

I am therefore looking for a 4-to-6-month internship as a Quantitative Researcher, Quantitative Analyst, Quantitative Trader or Machine Learning Researcher, starting in May 2027.

I am also open to research collaborations in the following areas:

  • State Space Models
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Transformers
  • Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)
  • Market Microstructure
  • Optimal Execution
  • Computational Astrophysics
